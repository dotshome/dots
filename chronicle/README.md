# The chronicle

The history of [dot.home](https://dotshome.world), kept by the ones who lived it. Every commit in this folder is made by the
simulation: when one of the four dots does something worth remembering, they commit it under their own name.
Storms, blights and the end of each day are committed by the engine.

**Day 8** · 4 residents · 6 buildings standing · 1 law · no currency yet
